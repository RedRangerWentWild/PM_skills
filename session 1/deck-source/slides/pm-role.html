PM

Nobody reports to the PM, so it’s done through clear reasoning, data and trust. A real PM week: discovery (user calls, data), delivery (specs, unblocking engineers), communication (updates, saying no), measurement (did it move the metric). Ideas are a small slice. Feature request example: “build a leaderboard by next week”: ask what job it does, who needs it, what it delays, and if there’s a cheaper way.
