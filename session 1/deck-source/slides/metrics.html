Match the metric to how often people use the product: daily users fits WhatsApp, not a travel app used a few times a year. Vanity check: if this number doubled, would users or the business definitely be better off? Downloads usually fail. Metric per lens: business (conversion, order value), user (task success, day-7 retention), tech (load time, payment success).
