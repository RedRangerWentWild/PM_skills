02 · Start with the user

Define the persona first

One person. One situation.

✕

“College students, 18 to 25” (too broad)

✓

“A hostel student ordering dinner at 11 PM with ₹200 left this week”

R

Riya, 19

2nd-year student, lives in a hostel

Example persona

Goal

Eat something decent without leaving her room during exams

Context

Late night, phone in one hand, notes in the other

Constraint

₹200 left this week; hates surprise fees

Behaviour

Reorders what she knows; sorts by price

Pain

The price she sees isn’t the price she pays

A good persona = who + goal + context + constraint. Different personas have different jobs, so mixing them dilutes every insight. Riya is made up for teaching; in a real breakdown, build the persona from reviews, a few user chats, or your own honest use.
