Layers: functional (order food), emotional (feel in control, no surprises), social (look smart to friends). Classic example: commuters “hired” a morning milkshake to make a boring drive less boring and stay full till lunch; the competition was bananas and bagels, not other milkshakes. McDonald's kiosk job: “I'm hungry. Let me order fast and eat.”
