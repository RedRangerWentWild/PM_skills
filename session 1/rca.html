What to hand in

1 to 2 pages, any format
  1. The problem in one line: who, what changed, by how much, since when.
  2. Your hypotheses, at least four. Mark each one confirmed, ruled out or unclear.
  3. The evidence behind each call.
  4. The root cause, traced until you reach something the team can change.
  5. One fix: change, metric, guardrail, why.
  6. What you observed vs what you assumed.
  7. One question you'd want answered before shipping.
15

NarrowingFinds exactly who and where, not "submissions dipped"

20

HypothesesInternal and external causes, each tested

20

Ruling outKills wrong causes with data, not opinion

20

Root causeLands on a specific product decision

15

The fixMetric and guardrail tied to the cause

10

HonestySeparates observed from assumed
